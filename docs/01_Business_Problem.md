# Enterprise Data Governance & Data Quality Implementation

## Company Background

RetailMax Ltd. is a fictional multinational retail company specializing in consumer electronics, home appliances, and lifestyle products. The company operates both physical retail stores and an e-commerce platform across multiple regions.

As the business expanded, several independent systems were introduced to support different business functions, including Customer Relationship Management (CRM), Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), Point of Sale (POS), Inventory Management, Supplier Management, and E-commerce.

While these systems improved operational efficiency, they also introduced inconsistencies in the way business data was captured, stored, and managed.

---

# Business Problem

RetailMax currently faces significant challenges in maintaining trusted, high-quality enterprise data.

The organization has experienced:

- Duplicate customer records across multiple systems
- Missing and incomplete customer information
- Inconsistent product descriptions and categories
- Conflicting sales figures between operational systems
- Lack of standardized business definitions
- No formal ownership of critical business data
- Manual effort required to validate reports before publication

These issues have reduced confidence in reporting, delayed decision-making, increased operational costs, and created compliance risks.

---

# Project Objective

The objective of this project is to design and implement an Enterprise Data Governance and Data Quality Framework that establishes governance standards, improves data quality, defines ownership, and enables trusted business reporting.

---

# Project Scope

This implementation covers the following business domains:

- Customer
- Product
- Sales
- Inventory
- Supplier

The project includes:

- Current State Assessment
- Stakeholder Analysis
- Data Inventory
- Business Glossary
- Data Dictionary
- Data Ownership Model
- Data Classification
- Metadata Management
- Data Lineage
- Data Quality Framework
- SQL Data Quality Validation Rules
- Governance Policies
- Power BI Data Quality Dashboard

---

# Expected Business Outcomes

- Improve data accuracy and completeness
- Reduce duplicate records
- Establish clear ownership of enterprise data
- Standardize business terminology
- Increase trust in reporting and analytics
- Support regulatory compliance
- Enable better business decision-making
