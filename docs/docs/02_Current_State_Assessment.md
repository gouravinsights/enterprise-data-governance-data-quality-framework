# Current State Assessment

## Purpose

Before designing an Enterprise Data Governance Framework, it is essential to understand the organization's current data landscape, governance maturity, and existing challenges.

This assessment evaluates how data is currently created, stored, shared, and consumed across RetailMax Ltd. The findings will serve as the foundation for designing governance processes, assigning ownership, improving data quality, and implementing enterprise-wide standards.

---

# Current Business Systems

RetailMax relies on several independent applications to support its business operations.

| Business System | Purpose | Primary Data |
|-----------------|---------|--------------|
| CRM | Customer Relationship Management | Customer Profiles |
| ERP | Finance & Procurement | Financial & Supplier Data |
| POS | Store Transactions | Sales Transactions |
| E-Commerce Platform | Online Sales | Orders & Customers |
| Inventory Management | Warehouse Operations | Inventory & Stock |
| Supplier Portal | Vendor Management | Supplier Information |

Although these systems support different business functions, they operate independently with limited integration and inconsistent data standards.

---

# Current Challenges

The assessment identified several governance and data quality issues.

## Data Quality

- Duplicate customer records
- Missing customer email addresses
- Invalid phone numbers
- Inconsistent product names
- Missing product categories
- Incorrect sales dates
- Negative sales transactions
- Incomplete supplier information

## Governance

- No formal Data Governance program
- No assigned Data Owners
- No Data Stewards
- No Business Glossary
- No Data Dictionary
- No enterprise Data Quality standards
- Limited metadata documentation

## Reporting

Business reports require significant manual validation before publication, resulting in delayed reporting cycles and reduced confidence in business metrics.

---

# Business Risks

The current operating model introduces several risks.

| Risk | Potential Impact |
|------|------------------|
| Duplicate Customers | Incorrect customer analytics |
| Poor Product Data | Inventory inaccuracies |
| Missing Data | Reduced reporting accuracy |
| Manual Validation | Increased operational effort |
| Undefined Ownership | Lack of accountability |
| Inconsistent Definitions | Conflicting KPIs across departments |

---

# Assessment Summary

The current assessment indicates that RetailMax Ltd. has reached a level of operational complexity where informal data management practices are no longer sufficient.

To support future business growth, the organization requires a structured Enterprise Data Governance Framework with clearly defined ownership, standardized metadata, enterprise-wide data quality controls, governance policies, and continuous monitoring.
